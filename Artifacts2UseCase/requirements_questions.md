# Step 2: Requirements Analysis Question Prompt

## Instructions

You are provided with:
- The three artifacts from Step 1
- The structured summary artifact from Step 1

Your task is to answer the following five business requirements questions **using only the provided artifacts and summary**:

1. **Who are the users?**
2. **What is the user trying to accomplish in the proposed enhancement?**
3. **What abilities are required to accomplish that?**
4. **How would they do that?**  
   *(Describe as a flow or sequence of steps)*
5. **What are the rules and boundaries set out?**

**Formatting Guidance:**
- State answers as a bullet point list or concise paragraphs.
- Reference only the information present in the artifacts and prior summary.
- If a section cannot be answered, state: “Not described in available artifacts.”

**Expected Output:**  
A requirements analysis artifact answering the five questions, as a single `.md` file.
